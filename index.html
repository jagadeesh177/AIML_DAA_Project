Step 3DAA: Minimum Stations Required (Binary Search)

Why Binary Search? Coverage is monotonically non-decreasing (\(C(k+1) \ge C(k)\)). Instead of 20 sequential runs (\(O(N)\)), binary search finds the exact minimum in at most 5 checks (\(O(\log N)\)).
